User Guide

Huz Trader Website Guide

Welcome! This guide explains how to use the Huz Trader website — both as a visitor browsing cars and as the administrator managing inventory.

🚗 For Visitors — Browsing the Website

What can visitors do?

Browse Cars

View all available vehicles with photos, pricing, and specifications.

Recently Sold

See vehicles that have already been sold.

Car Enquiries

Contact Huz Trader regarding a specific vehicle.

Import Requests

Request a quote to source a vehicle directly from Japan.

General Contact

Send questions through the website contact page.

How does enquiring work?

  1. The visitor clicks Enquire on a vehicle.
  2. They enter their name, email, phone number, and message.
  3. The enquiry is submitted.
What happens next?
  1. You receive an email containing the enquiry.
  2. The customer receives a confirmation email.
  3. The enquiry is stored in the admin panel.

🔐 For the Admin — Managing Your Website

How to log in

  1. Go to your website and add /admin/login to the URL.
  2. Enter your email and password.
  3. Click Sign In.
https://huztrader.vercel.app/admin/login
After signing in, you'll be taken to the Cars Management page.

➕ How to Add a Car

  1. Click the Add Car button in the admin panel.
  2. Complete the vehicle details.
  3. Upload photos.
  4. Click Add Car to publish immediately.

Basic Information

  • Make: Toyota
  • Model: Supra
  • Variant: GR Sport / Turbo
  • Year: 2020
  • Status: For Sale
  • Price: 45000
  • Mileage: 35000 km
  • Stock #: S001 (optional)

Specifications

  • Body Type
  • Transmission
  • Fuel Type
  • Colour
  • VIN (optional)
  • Import Vehicle Checkbox

Description

Include features, service history, condition details, and any additional information buyers should know.

Photos

  • Upload one or more images.
  • The first image becomes the listing cover photo.
  • Additional photos appear in the vehicle gallery.
  • Hover and click ✕ to remove a photo.

✏️ How to Edit a Car

  1. Navigate to Admin → Cars.
  2. Click the pencil icon.
  3. Update the information.
  4. Click Save Changes.

✅ How to Mark a Car as Sold

  1. Go to Admin → Cars.
  2. Edit the vehicle.
  3. Change Status from For Sale to Sold.
  4. Click Save Changes.
Result:
  • The vehicle disappears from Cars For Sale.
  • It automatically appears on the Recently Sold page.

🗑️ How to Delete a Car

  1. Go to Admin → Cars.
  2. Click the bin icon.
  3. Confirm deletion.
Warning: Deleting a vehicle is permanent. If you only want to remove it temporarily, change its status to Reserved instead.

📧 How to View Enquiries

  1. Go to Admin → Enquiries.
  2. View enquiries ordered from newest to oldest.
  3. Click an email address to reply directly.

Each enquiry includes:

  • Customer name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Message
  • Vehicle details (if applicable)
  • Budget and location (for import requests)
You also receive every enquiry by email immediately after submission.

🚪 How to Sign Out

Click Sign Out at the bottom of the left sidebar.

💡 Tips

  • Photos Matter: Aim for 5–8 clear photos showing exterior, interior, engine bay, and notable features.
  • Prices: Enter numbers only. Example: 45000 not $45,000.
  • Descriptions: Include service history, roadworthy information, recent work, and finance options.
  • Replying: Simply reply to enquiry emails and your response goes directly to the customer.
